## What this branch does

The home page was rebuilt as a **proposal in six numbered sections**, typeset like an
audit report. The site sells an independent technical review to a non-technical founder
who arrives from Upwork, and the old page looked like a SaaS template — its design system
aimed itself at Stripe while its Tailwind tokens were named after Zapier.

The binding constraint throughout: **build credibility with no social proof.** There are
no testimonials, no logos, no case studies, and there will not be until the first paid
audit. Section 04 shows the artifact a client receives instead of claiming to have one.

Design and plan: `docs/plans/2026-08-26-proposal-home-redesign-design.md` and
`docs/plans/2026-08-26-proposal-home-implementation.md`.

## Decisions worth not re-litigating

- **`fmv` is the service, `wakeymoment` is the company.** Not interchangeable. The service
  leads wherever a visitor is deciding whether to buy; the company owns the copyright line,
  the about page, and the Organization and publisher nodes in the structured data.
- **Colour means a verdict.** The brand purple was dropped rather than renamed, and so was
  an editorial orange that had been sitting 15° from `p0` on the hue wheel. Links and
  buttons are ink. The one documented exception is the logo mark's red dot, which is
  identity rather than a judgement — `DESIGN.md` records why.
- **No gate count on the page.** The pricing document sells "twelve gates", the gate
  document defines seven, two of those are alternatives. No number is verified, so none
  appears. Two tests guard it, scanning rendered HTML including attributes.
- **The nav links only pages that carry receipts.** `/services` was deleted rather than
  linked because it was 4,534 words of Korean on a site whose buyer reads English.
- **No card shells.** Sections and article blocks are separated by rules, not by rounded
  white boxes floating on the paper ground.

## Things that will bite you

- **`agent-browser` attaches to the user's real Chrome.** It hijacks their tab, reloads it
  under them, and its screenshots can capture whatever they were looking at. Verify pages
  with `curl` instead. This cost an hour of the session and looked like a scroll bug.
- **The dev server 500s in a sandbox** because `next/font/google` cannot reach
  `fonts.gstatic.com`. Not a code problem.
- **`pnpm build`'s contentlayer step prints a TypeError every run** and exits 0 anyway.
  Pre-existing on `master`; contentlayer is archived upstream.
- **`.underline` and `.decoration-*` utilities are not generated by this build.** An anchor
  given `class="underline"` computes to `text-decoration-line: none`. `TextLink` is
  underlined with a border instead. Root cause never found — worth a look.
- **Drafts are filtered from listings but their routes still answer 200 in dev.**
  `src/lib/post-access.ts`.
