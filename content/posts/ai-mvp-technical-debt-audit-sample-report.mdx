<ReportSection number="12" title="Go / No-Go recommendation">

**No-Go for public launch. Conditional Go for private pilot** only after P0
risks are verified and P1 issues are bounded.

<ReportTable
  caption="Decision options"
  headers={["Decision", "When it applies"]}
  verdictColumns={[0]}
  rows={[
    ["No-Go", "P0 security/data risks are unresolved."],
    ["Conditional", "Core flow works, P0 risks are handled, and remaining risks are documented."],
    ["Go", "Auth/data, build/check, rollback, and UX reliability gates are all verified."],
  ]}
/>

Your MVP is not "bad." It is doing what AI-built prototypes often do: it proves
the idea faster than it proves the system. The next step is not to rebuild
everything. The next step is to contain the highest-risk failure points before
real users, real data, and real customer trust are involved.
